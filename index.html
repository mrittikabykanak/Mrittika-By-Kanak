the hands behind Mrittika by Kanak

Hi, I'm Kanak

I'm a working professional based in Mumbai, married, and someone who has always found her way back to art — no matter how full the week gets. Clay is the medium I've settled into, mostly because it doesn't ask me to choose between being precise and being playful. It lets me be both.

Mrittika is how I get to bring that side of myself out into the open, and more than that, it's how I get to bring it out in other people too. Every custom piece starts with someone telling me about a person they love — their humour, their quirks, the things that make them them — and my job is to shape that into something small enough to hold. That's the part that keeps me coming back to the worktable every evening: it's rarely just about the clay, it's about the person it ends up with.
